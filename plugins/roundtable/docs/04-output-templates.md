# Output Templates

## Roundtable Summary Template

Use this template at the end of every roundtable session (Phase 5: Synthesis). Fill every section — mark sections "None" if truly empty rather than omitting them.

---

```markdown
# Roundtable Summary: [Topic]

**Date:** [Date]
**Participants:**
- **Experts:** [Name (Domain), ...]
- **Personas:** [Name (Role), ...]

---

## Executive Summary

[2-4 sentences capturing the key outcome. What was discussed? What emerged? What's the main recommendation?]

---

## Expert Insights

### [Expert Name] — [Domain]
- [Key insight 1]
- [Key insight 2]
- [Key constraint or opportunity noted]

[Repeat for each expert]

---

## Persona Perspectives

### [Persona Name] — [Role]
- **Reaction:** [Emotional response and key concerns]
- **Key need:** [What they want most]
- **Quote:** "[Most memorable thing they said]"

[Repeat for each persona]

---

## Prioritized Feature Ideas

| # | Idea | Impact | Effort | Priority | Champion |
|---|------|--------|--------|----------|----------|
| 1 | [Idea description] | High/Med/Low | High/Med/Low | Must/Should/Could | [Who advocated] |
| 2 | ... | ... | ... | ... | ... |

---

## UX Recommendations

1. **[Recommendation title]** — [Description. Which persona need does this address? Which expert insight supports it?]
2. ...

---

## Concerns & Risks

| Risk | Raised By | Severity | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|----------|------------|
| [Risk description] | [Participant] | High/Med/Low | [Suggested approach] |

---

## Consensus Points

Things everyone agreed on:
1. [Point]
2. ...

## Disagreements

Areas where perspectives diverged (these are valuable signals):
1. **[Topic]** — [Expert/Persona A] believes [X], while [Expert/Persona B] believes [Y]. Implication: [what this tension means for the product].
2. ...

---

## Action Items

| # | Action | Owner | Notes |
|---|--------|-------|-------|
| 1 | [Specific next step] | [Suggested owner or team] | [Context] |
| 2 | ... | ... | ... |

---

## Open Questions

Questions that surfaced but weren't resolved:
1. [Question]
2. ...

## Parking Lot

Ideas or topics worth exploring in a future session:
1. [Topic]
2. ...
```

---

## Usage Notes

- **Champion column** in the feature table tracks which participant(s) advocated for the idea — useful for follow-up
- **Disagreements section is mandatory** — if all personas and experts agreed on everything, the BA should note this as a signal that the panel may lack diversity
- **Quotes** from personas make the summary more vivid and memorable for stakeholders reading it later
- **Parking lot** prevents scope creep while honoring ideas that surfaced — nothing is lost
- The BA may add a **"BA's Recommendation"** section at the end with their own synthesis if the user requests it
